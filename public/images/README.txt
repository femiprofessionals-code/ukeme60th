Drop these 6 files into this folder (exact names — the site references them):

  invitation.jpg   <- the invitation card (used in the hero + gallery feature)
  ukeme-1.jpg       <- "A Life of Grace"
  ukeme-2.jpg       <- "60 Years of Thanksgiving"

Tips:
- Portrait orientation (e.g. 1200 x 1500px) looks best in the frames.
- Keep each file under ~400KB for fast mobile loading (compress at tinypng.com).
- To change captions or filenames, edit src/lib/siteConfig.js (galleryImages).
- Until you add the files, the site shows tasteful gold placeholders — nothing breaks.

For social sharing previews, also keep invitation.jpg here: it is the Open Graph
image referenced in index.html.
