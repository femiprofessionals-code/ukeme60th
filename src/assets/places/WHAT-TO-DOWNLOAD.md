## What makes a good one

- **Landscape**, roughly 3:2. A portrait photograph will be cropped hard.
- **Subject in the upper half** — on a wide screen the crop comes off the
  bottom, and the lower part is dimmed so the text over it stays readable.
- **1600–2000px** on the long edge. Aim under 400 KB: JPEG at about 75%, or
  WebP.
- Wide establishing shots beat close-ups. This is a background, not a portrait.

## Where to get them

- **Unsplash** and **Pexels** — free to use commercially, no attribution
  required, and the quality is high. Easiest option.
- **Wikimedia Commons** — free but most photographs require crediting the
  photographer. If you use these, send the credit lines too and they can go in
  the page footer.

Please use photographs that are actually licensed for this — your own, or from
one of the sources above. Images pulled off a search results page usually are
not.
